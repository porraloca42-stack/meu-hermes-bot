# TikTok strategy

1. Identify the specific project, audience, offer and objective: reach, followers, watch time, clicks, leads or sales.
2. Keep distinct user projects and brands separate.
3. Audit the current baseline from supplied analytics; state missing data.
4. Map audience problem/desire, content promise, proof, differentiation and CTA.
5. Build content pillars and repeatable formats linked to the objective.
6. For each experiment define hypothesis, variable, control/baseline, sample, observation window and success/failure criteria.
7. Prioritize low-cost tests and avoid changing many variables at once.
8. Do not promise virality or treat correlations as causes.
9. Present a ranked recommendation with rationale, risks and next action.
