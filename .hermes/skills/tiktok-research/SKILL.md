# TikTok research

1. Restate the research question and the decision it should inform.
2. Use the available `web_search` and `web_extract` tools for current research. If the managed/free web providers are rate-limited, retry once through an available keyless fallback or DuckDuckGo skill. If no search path is available, state the limitation and do not invent current facts or sources.
3. Search current primary sources first: TikTok Newsroom, TikTok Help Center, TikTok Creative Center, official product/creator documentation, public regulatory sources and original research.
4. Add reputable independent reporting or research where useful. For trends, establish the date range, market and evidence of momentum.
5. Record source title, publisher, publication/update date, URL, relevant evidence and limitations.
6. Separate direct observations, sourced facts, interpretation and hypotheses.
7. Never infer a trend from one viral post or invent views, sales, retention or audience demographics.
8. End with practical implications, confidence level and what should be tested next.
9. If sources disagree, show the disagreement rather than forcing a conclusion.
