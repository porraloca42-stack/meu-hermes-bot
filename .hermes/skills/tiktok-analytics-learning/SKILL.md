# TikTok analytics and learning

1. Request or inspect actual metrics and their time window: views, average watch time, retention curve, completion, rewatches, likes, comments, shares, saves, profile visits, follows, clicks and orders where available.
2. Record video duration, publish time, format, topic, hook, CTA, paid promotion and audience targeting. Separate organic from paid distribution.
3. Normalize comparisons where possible (e.g. average watch time relative to video length); do not compare unlike posts as if controlled.
4. Distinguish observation from causal explanation. One post is a signal, not proof.
5. Identify likely bottlenecks and rank hypotheses by evidence and testability.
6. Recommend the smallest next experiment that can discriminate between hypotheses.
7. Maintain an experiment log: date, project, hypothesis, creative variables, metrics, interpretation, confidence and next action.
8. Promote a lesson into persistent memory or a skill only if it is durable and supported by repeated evidence; preserve counterexamples and failed tests.
