# Source and claim audit

1. Extract each factual, statistical, scientific, product and platform-policy claim.
2. Find the strongest available primary source for each claim; verify the source actually supports the exact wording.
3. Record date, scope, population/context and limitations.
4. Flag unsupported causal claims, exaggerated certainty, misleading omissions and outdated information.
5. For quantum physics, distinguish established physical theory from metaphorical or spiritual interpretations. Never claim that quantum mechanics proves manifestation or guarantees personal outcomes.
6. For commercial content, verify product details against the seller/manufacturer listing and avoid inventing features, testimonials, discounts or scarcity.
7. Classify each claim: verified, qualified, unsupported, contradicted or opinion.
8. Rewrite or remove claims that cannot be supported. Provide citations and a short risk summary.
